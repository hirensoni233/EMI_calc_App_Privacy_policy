Privacy Policy for EMI Calculator Pro

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Summary in Plain English

EMI Calculator Pro is designed with a strict zero-data-collection philosophy. All loan computations, financial parameters, amortization schedules, and comparison scenarios operate 100% locally on your device. We do not require account registration, collect personal data, track your activity, or transmit your financial inputs to any server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because EMI Calculator Pro is an offline mathematical calculator and comparison tool, we do not collect, store, process, or sell any personal or financial information. Specifically:

  • No Financial Data: Your loan amounts, interest rates, salary figures, existing loan liabilities, down payments, and calculated EMIs remain strictly on your local device.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, device identifiers (IMEI/Android ID), or account credentials.
  • No Location Data: The application does not access GPS or approximate network location.
  • No Camera or Media Access: The application does not read or inspect your private photo gallery, audio, or internal device storage.

2. On-Device Data Storage & Preferences

Any settings you adjust inside the application (such as preferred currency symbol, Indian vs. International numbering system, default loan term, or dark theme preferences) are stored strictly on your local device storage using standard Android sandbox storage.

This data is never transmitted to any cloud servers or third parties and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the application or clear its application data via Android System Settings.

3. Device Permissions & Network Usage

The application only declares minimum standard Android permissions necessary for basic app functionality:

  • INTERNET: Only used if you choose to load informational visual assets or if you initiate a voluntary external share dialog (such as sharing an amortization schedule or report via your own installed messaging apps or email clients). We do not run any background tracking or analytics servers.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

EMI Calculator Pro does not contain:

  • No third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Adjust, Mixpanel).
  • No advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook Audience Network).
  • No user tracking mechanisms, cookies, or profiling systems.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is a general-purpose financial calculation utility. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

6. Google Play Developer Policy Compliance

This application strictly complies with Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data policy and Permissions guidelines. Because no user data is collected or shared, your usage of EMI Calculator Pro is completely anonymous and private.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future software updates or regulatory adjustments. Any updates will be published with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document.
